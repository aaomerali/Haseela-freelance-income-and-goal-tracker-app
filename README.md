# 💰 Haseela (حصيلة)
### Freelance Income & Goal Tracker

**Haseela** is a dedicated financial management tool designed specifically for freelancers. It simplifies tracking income, managing clients, and monitoring monthly financial goals, moving you away from complex spreadsheets to a streamlined, intuitive dashboard.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

* **📊 Financial Dashboard:** Get a bird's-eye view of your monthly earnings and progress toward your goals.
* **🎯 Goal Monitoring:** Set specific financial targets and visualize your achievement percentage in real-time.
* **👥 Client Management:** Maintain a professional directory of your clients and link them to specific projects.
* **📝 Task-Based Earnings:** Log income at the task level for granular tracking of your time and effort.
* **📱 Responsive Design:** Fully optimized for both desktop and mobile use using Tailwind CSS.
